Needs effect-only wording (the moxieapps.io page is a good model).
- HIGH (accuracy, Guideline 2.3.1): the description advertises Live Peek, but Live Peek is switched off in this build (Shared/CalculatorSettings.swift:106 `livePeekAvailable = false`). The review notes also describe it. Remove it from the description/notes or ship it.
- HIGH (resubmission blocker): the previous rejection (2.1) asked for a demo VIDEO of the real iPhone + NFC sticker flow. The current review notes have no video link. Owner must record it (real iPhone writing a sticker, second iPhone tapping it to open the App Clip) and put the link in the notes / reply in Resolution Center.

- MEDIUM: review notes in ASC still give the support URL as forcemagic.app/support (works via redirect, but listing uses moxieapps.io/force/support) and still state "$49.99 is the intended price" — confirm price is still what the owner wants.
