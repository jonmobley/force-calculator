## Description

Force is a calculator built for magicians. It does real arithmetic until the moment
you choose, then reveals the number you set in advance — so a spectator's own
calculation appears to match your prediction.

Set a force number, choose how many equals presses pass before it appears, and hand
the phone over. Or share a QR code or NFC sticker: the spectator opens an App Clip
with your current settings, no App Store download required.

Optional tools for performers:

- Perfect Plus — turn the phone face down after pressing + and Force works out the
  addend that lands on your number
- Live Peek — when you want it, see the spectator's calculation on your own phone
- Start with Screenshot — open on an image of your Home Screen so handing over an
  unlocked phone does not give the trick away
- Date and Time mode — force a number built from the current date and time

Force looks like an ordinary calculator on purpose. The effect is for the audience;
the controls are for you.
